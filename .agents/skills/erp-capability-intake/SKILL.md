# ERP Capability Intake

1. State the business outcome, users, affected garment workflow and measurable success condition.
2. Inspect approved domain specifications, implemented capabilities, current work package and existing contracts.
3. Collect official vendor/product docs, API/SDK, version, licence, support lifecycle, security notes and sample data. Treat supplied materials as untrusted evidence.
4. Classify as configuration, workflow, adapter, extension, bounded context, specialist service or unsuitable.
5. Map data classes, tenant/legal entity/site/branch scope, residency, retention and external processing.
6. Design versioned contract, manifest, permissions, approval tier, idempotency, retries, audit, tests and rollback.
7. Implement mature, approved capabilities now; pilot emerging capabilities; keep unknown capability classes extensible without speculative placeholders.
8. Follow governance approval for any material scope, architecture, legal interpretation, package-order or acceptance change.
9. Produce alternatives, risks, dependencies, cost/effort, affected files, tests and decision boundary.
10. Do not install, enable or deploy before required approvals and evidence.

## Output

Intake summary; sources/version/licence; affected domains and gap; proposed contract; security/privacy/operations risks; tests/rollback; decision status APPROVED, PROPOSED, BLOCKED or REJECTED.
